How the score is counted

  • The headline is the mean of per-feature parity, so each feature weighs the same however many cases it has.
  • A case tagged with several features counts once in each of them; a case without flags counts in base.
  • Self-description features are reported on their own and left out of the headline.
  • Features with fewer than 20 scored cases are under-sampled: shown, but left out of the headline.
  • Waived cases are left out of both sides of the parity, never counted as passes.
  • A flaky candidate or a candidate timeout counts as a divergence; a case where the reference itself is flaky is left out.
